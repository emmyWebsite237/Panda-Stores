⚙️ How we work

Three rules we do not break

They are short, and they are the reason people come back.

Rule 01

Nothing gets listed unchecked

If we cannot open the account and screenshot the inventory, it does not go live. No borrowed images, no “trust me” listings.

Rule 02

The price you see is the price

No surprise fees at checkout, no renegotiating on WhatsApp. The exchange rate is published and applied the same way for everyone.

Rule 03

We stay on chat after payment

You get 24 hours of support to change the email, set recovery options and secure the account properly. Then it is genuinely yours.

Send us the account details and screenshots — we make a fair offer, usually the same day.
