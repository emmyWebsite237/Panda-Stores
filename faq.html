❓ Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything buyers and sellers ask before their first deal. If your question is not here, message us — we usually reply in under 10 minutes.

How do I actually buy an account?

Open the shop, pick a listing and tap the WhatsApp buy button. The purchase terms open first — read them, then confirm. That opens WhatsApp with the account name, price and delivery code already filled in. Pay, and the credentials are released on the same chat.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment details are sent on WhatsApp once you confirm the order — bank transfer is the usual route, and the exact amount in Naira is calculated at the store rate shown on the site.

We never ask for payment through a link, and we never ask for your account password before delivery.

Why is the price shown in both Naira and USD?

The USD figure is the real listing price. The Naira figure is that price converted at the store rate, so you always know roughly what you will pay before you message us. The rate is published on the site and set in the admin dashboard.

Naira price is calculated at the store rate. Final amount is confirmed on WhatsApp.

How fast is delivery?

Usually minutes. Once payment is confirmed the login details go out on the same chat, and we stay with you while you change the email and recovery settings. Mon – Sat · 9:00am – 9:00pm is when someone is always at the keyboard.

Is the account really mine after I pay?

Yes. You change the email address and recovery options during the handover, while we are both on the chat. Once that is done the account cannot be pulled back — and we keep 24 hours of support open in case something does not behave.

What if the account does not match the listing?

Message the same chat within 24 hours. If the inventory does not match what was advertised, or the account cannot be delivered at all, you get a refund. Change of mind is not a refund reason — the full terms explain it in detail.

Can I sell my account here?

Definitely. Use the sell form — add the game, rank, level, your asking price and screenshots of the inventory. We review it and come back with an offer, usually the same day. If you accept, we transfer on WhatsApp and pay on handover.

Do you buy accounts with an unchangeable email?

Usually not. If the original email cannot be changed or recovered, the account is not safely transferable, so we either decline it or offer a much lower price. Tell us up front in the sell form and we will be straight with you.

I cannot find the game or rank I want. Can you source it?

Yes — this is one of the most common requests. Send the game, the rank or inventory you are after and your budget through the contact form or on WhatsApp, and we will look for it and come back with options.

Are there age requirements?

You need to be 18 or over, or have permission from a parent or guardian, to buy or sell an account here. Gaming platform terms also apply — read them before you buy.
