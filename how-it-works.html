⚡ The full process

How it works, start to finish

No forms to fill, no waiting rooms, no mystery steps. Here is exactly what happens — and how long each part takes.

🛒 Buying an account

Four steps, usually under ten minutes

01

Pick the account

Open the shop and filter by game, price or availability. Each listing shows the real inventory — level, rank, skins, pets — with screenshots, so you know exactly what you are buying before you commit.

Takes about 2 minutes · Go to the shop →
02

Read the terms, then confirm

Tap the WhatsApp buy button and the purchase terms open first. The “I Agree” button stays disabled for a few seconds on purpose — we want you to actually read them. Confirming unlocks the delivery code tied to that specific account.

Takes about 30 seconds · Read the terms →
03

Message us on WhatsApp

One tap opens WhatsApp with the account name, price and delivery code already written into the message. Nothing to copy, nothing to retype. We reply with payment details and confirm the amount in Naira at the store rate.

Replies typically under 10 minutes
04

Pay, receive, secure

Once payment is confirmed we send the login details and stay on the chat while you change the email and set up recovery. You get 24 hours of support afterwards for anything that comes up.

Handover in minutes · 24 hours of support
💸 Selling an account

Turn an idle account into cash

01

Send the details

Fill in the sell form with the game, rank, level and what you want for it. Attach screenshots of the inventory — the clearer the proof, the faster the offer. You can also just message us on WhatsApp.

Takes about 3 minutes · Start a submission →
02

Get a fair offer

We review the account and come back with a price, usually the same day. If the inventory is worth more than you expected, we will say so — we would rather pay correctly than lose the account.

Typically same-day
03

Accept, hand over, get paid

Accept the offer and we handle the transfer on WhatsApp: you keep the account until payment lands, then we take it over, secure it and list it. Ownership shifts cleanly, with no loose ends.

Payment on transfer
🔒 Security

How we keep the handover safe

Before payment

Everything is shown up front

Inventory, level, rank and screenshots are on the listing. You are not buying a promise, you are buying what you can already see.

During handover

You change the details, on chat

The email and recovery options are changed while we are both on the conversation, so nobody can take the account back later.

After delivery

24 hours of cover

If anything looks wrong in the first day, message the same chat. We fix it or refund it — the terms spell out exactly when a refund applies.
